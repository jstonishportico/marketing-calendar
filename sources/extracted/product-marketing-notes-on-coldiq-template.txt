Financial Aid Services & Software
Core Problem(s)
Schools needing Financial Aid Services
Staffing & Expertise Gaps – Schools struggle with hiring, training, and retaining in-house financial aid staff, and it’s hard to quickly scale staffing
Compliance Risks – Staying up-to-date with federal and state regulations is complicated and time-consuming
Seasonal Workload Spikes – Peaks during enrollment periods create inefficiencies and slow processing times
Slow Processing Times – Internal bottlenecks delay ISIR corrections, document verification, and fund disbursement
Audits & Reconciliation Challenges – Institutions need expert guidance to manage reconciliation and compliance issues
Schools needing Financial Aid Software
Manual, Inefficient Processes – Time-consuming processes such as FAFSA/ISIR import, document verification, award packaging, and fund disbursement can be slow if they are not automated, or current software don’t support seamless processing
Lack of Software Customization – Most FA tools aren’t flexible enough to support various academic models (clock hour, credit hour, hybrid)
Disjointed Tech Stack – FA operations require multiple logins across government and internal systems
Unique Solution
Portico Financial Aid simplifies financial aid management by combining automation, expert services, and compliance support into one seamless solution. Whether schools need full-service outsourcing, software, or project-based assistance, we reduce manual work, ensure compliance, and improve efficiency—so they can focus on student success.

Attendance Product
Core Problem(s)
Too Much Manual Work – Schools rely on paper-based attendance and skills tracking, leading to errors, delays, and compliance risks
Retention Struggles – Lack of real-time student attendance data makes it hard to track at-risk students and intervene early, leading to preventable dropouts
Compliance & Accreditation Headaches – Schools need accurate, up-to-date LDA, engagement verification, and digital records to meet Dept. of Education guidelines and pass audits
Disjointed Student Experience – Students rarely use outdated SIS portals; they expect a mobile app for attendance, payments, skills tracking, and coursework in one place
Disconnected Systems – Schools juggle multiple tools for attendance, skills tracking, surveys, student interventions, and payments, creating inefficiencies and data silos
No Automation – Schools waste time and resources manually tracking attendance, engagement, and payments, increasing compliance risks and operational costs
Unique Solution
Portico’s mobile Attendance & Skills solution gives learners a convenient place to manage attendance, chart progress, and stay on track.
Improve Retention through Automation – Use real-time student data and automated nudges to boost retention by 3.5% on average—no IT resources required
Improve Compliance– Digitize paper processes, ensure data accuracy, and flag compliance risks before they become audit issues
Better Student Experience – Students can clock in, track skills, view LMS/SIS data, and make payments in one easy-to-use mobile app
Consolidate Your Tech Stack – Replace multiple tools for attendance, skills tracking, surveys, externships, and payments, reducing manual work and costs
Time Savings & Efficiency – Automates outdated processes like manual data entry, student communications, and student monitoring to improve retention, as well as paper-based attendance
Patented Attendance Solution – Patented GPS attendance tool ensures fraud-proof, real-time attendance tracking, even for asynchronous and blended learning
