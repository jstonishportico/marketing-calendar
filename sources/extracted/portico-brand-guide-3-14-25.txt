1.0 Introduction
Purpose of This Guide
This guide will help you understand 
our brand – how we built it, how  
it looks and feels today, and how it  
applies to you and your work.
Whether you’re a new employee or one of our partners, working on product 
updates or writing a blog post, this guide will help ensure that the Portico 
brand is used consistently – everywhere, every time. We aren’t the “brand 
police” but we do care deeply about our core values and how they come 
through in everything that has our name on it.

2.0 Logo
Meaning
Our logo features three keystones – like the 
central stone in an arch, forming the heart  
of a portico. These keystones represent  
our role in supporting student success.  
They also symbolize the power of threes  
in our story: the three founding companies, 
values, and stakeholders (students, schools, 
and employers). And that lowercase slab 
serif wordmark? It’s a reflection of our human 
touch, built on trust and support.
Our logos can be downloaded here.

2.1 Logo
Secondary  
Expression
In certain contexts, such as limited color  
usage or small-scale applications, we rely on 
an outline version of the mark. This simplified 
line art preserves our core brand identity while 
ensuring legibility in restrictive formats. Use 
the outline version whenever color options are 
minimal, or when the design needs to maintain 
clarity at a reduced size.

4.0 Patterns
Primary Brand Pattern
Our brand pattern is derived from the portico mark. This pattern is intentionally subtle, using colors from our  
neutral palette to add texture and depth without overwhelming the design. It serves as a versatile background or 
accent, reinforcing our brand’s visual language while maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic across all applications. 
The patterns can be accessed here. 

Page 16:
4.1 Patterns
Secondary Brand Pattern
Our secondary brand pattern builds on the main brand pattern, adding a dynamic twist with selective trapezoids filled 
in with bright colors from our primary palette. These vibrant accents are used sparingly, occupying no more than 20% 
of the overall composition, to ensure the pattern remains more subtle. This approach adds visual interest and energy 
while preserving the clean, supportive structure that defines our brand identity.

5.0 Typography
Primary Typeface
Proxima Nova Regular is the keystone of 
our brand’s typography and is used for 
both headlines and body copy, delivering 
clarity and a modern feel. Proxima Nova 
Bold is ideal for key messages and calls-
to-action, while Proxima Nova Italics add 
nuance and emphasis where needed.  
This consistent typographic approach 
ensures that our communications remain 
clean, approachable, and impactful.

5.2 Typography
System Typefaces
When our custom fonts aren’t available 
due to technical limitations or licensing 
restrictions, it’s essential to use approved 
system typefaces as reliable fallbacks. 
When Proxima Nova isn’t available,  
Arial should be used. This offers a clean,  
modern sans-serif look that loosely  
resembles Proxima Nova for headlines  
and body copy.
If Hepta Slab is unavailable, Rockwell 
should be used. Rockwell provides a 
slab-serif appearance that maintains  
a similar strong and geometric character 
for subheads and calls-to-action.

6.0 Icons
Icon Style
Our icon style is an extension of our brand 
mark, crafted in a clean, outline (stroke) 
style. Each icon should use consistent line 
weights and minimal detailing to ensure  
clarity and coherence across all digital and 
print applications. While the primary  
emphasis is on the simplicity and legibility of 
the stroke, subtle accents from our approved 
color palette may be incorporated to  
enhance visual interest when needed.

7.1 Messaging & Positioning
Voice & Tone
Communication style:
Clear  
We’re simple, transparent, and honest.  
We work hard to build our customers’ 
trust. We explain complex concepts in 
plain language, avoiding technical jargon  
and making information accessible 
without sacrificing sophistication.  
When using industry terms, we provide 
context and explanation. We generally 
avoid over-used words that don't have 
much meaning themselves.
Thoughtful  
We’re discreet and take our customers’  
operations seriously. We demonstrate  
a deep understanding of their challenges 
while maintaining professional optimism. 
Our communication anticipates questions 
and provides relevant context without 
overwhelming detail. This voice cuts 
through complexity while maintaining 
authority, offering guidance rather  
than lectures. 
Tone:
Portico's voice embodies a trusted  
mentor who balances expertise  
with empathy. The trusted mentor  
personality is characterized as witty  
and casual while being respectful  
and matter-of-fact.
Human  
We understand the challenges you’re 
facing. We are approachable and caring. 
We recognize that educational technology  
is about more than just systems and 
data - it's about supporting real people 
facing real challenges. Our empathetic 
approach acknowledges the pressures 
of managing student success, staff  
turnover, and regulatory requirements.
Strategically Witty  
We use purposeful wit and relevant  
analogies to build trust and  
understanding. Our humor lightens 
complex discussions while respecting 
the gravity of our customers' challenges. 
Like a trusted advisor sharing insights 
over coffee, we know when to add  
levity and when to maintain focus. This  
approach makes technical concepts 
more approachable without diminishing 
their importance.
