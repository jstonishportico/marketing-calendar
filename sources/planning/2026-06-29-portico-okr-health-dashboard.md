## Health Legend

- **In progress:** meaningful work exists, but the OKR still needs launch, data, or adoption proof.
- **At risk:** the OKR depends on missing data, unclear definitions, or stakeholder alignment.

### 4. Inbound Demo Requests: 100

**Health:** At risk until "valid lead" is defined and counted.

**Evidence / receipts**

- User context: raw demo request submissions are not enough because the target should only count valid leads.
- Webinar demo requests should count when valid.
- Known signals include a handful of OBBBA Q&A demo requests and one from the Vet Tech webinar.
- Events and webinars are already producing follow-up opportunities, including CECU and Vet Tech.

**Missing proof**

- Final count of raw demo requests.
- Final count of valid demo requests.
- Definition of valid lead.
- Breakdown by source and product lane.
- Disqualification reasons.
- Sales handoff status and meeting outcome.

**Working valid lead definition**

Count a demo request when it is:

- From a real school, program, or relevant institution.
- From a buyer, influencer, or plausible internal champion.
- Relevant to a Portico product lane.
- Not spam, vendor outreach, student inquiry, job seeker, or duplicate.
- Routed to Sales, booked, nurtured, or explicitly disqualified with a reason.

**Suggested source buckets**

- Website demo request.
- Webinar demo request.
- Event follow-up request.
- ColdIQ reply.
- Paid landing page.
- Organic/content.
- Partner/referral.
- Sales-entered or manually sourced.

**Next move**

Audit H1 demo requests and webinar requests in one table: raw request, valid or invalid, product lane, source, next step, owner, and outcome. Report both numbers: raw submissions and valid leads.

**Dependency**

HubSpot/source data quality, webinar platform exports, sales routing, and valid lead criteria agreement.

**H2 treatment**

Change the metric from "demo requests" to "valid inbound leads and meetings by source." This makes the OKR harder, but much more useful.
