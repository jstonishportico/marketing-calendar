## Themes

1. **Measurement quality matters more than raw volume.** The inbound OKR cannot be judged by form submissions alone if many are not valid leads.
2. **ColdIQ is the H2 growth engine setup.** It should be framed as campaign infrastructure and ABM launch, not a one-off vendor onboarding task.
3. **Jessica's role is operating-system level.** The OKRs require source-of-truth reconciliation, campaign architecture, HubSpot/list discipline, Sales handoff, and reporting.
4. **H1 success should include setup work.** Some H1 work created the conditions for H2 impact, especially ColdIQ, core campaign lanes, and campaign measurement.

## Tensions

- **Activity vs. qualified demand:** Demo requests are easy to count, but valid leads are what leadership actually cares about.
- **Launch vs. impact:** ColdIQ/core product campaigns can be launched before pipeline/revenue impact is visible.
- **Individual ownership vs. shared systems:** Jessica can drive the marketing operating layer, but pipeline and lead validity depend on Sales/RevOps definitions and follow-through.
- **Planned OKR date vs. real recovery/workload:** The June 15 audit target slipped, but the work is still useful if completed with a stronger H2 plan this week.

   - Treat Financial Aid, Attendance, and Clinical/Trajecsys as the core campaign portfolio.
   - Define "launched" as lists/exclusions approved, copy/assets approved, routing confirmed, campaigns live, and weekly review cadence active.

2. **Create a valid lead and demo request scorecard.**
   - Count only qualified/valid leads.
   - Include webinar demo requests when they meet fit criteria.
   - Segment by product lane and source.

3. **Build a channel-to-pipeline reporting view.**
   - Partner with Sales/RevOps to connect HubSpot sources, campaign influence, webinar/event sources, and ColdIQ activity to pipeline.

4. **Turn webinars/events into conversion systems.**
   - Use OBBBA Q&A and Vet Tech as examples.
   - Track registrations, attendance, demo requests, valid leads, meetings, sales handoff, and influenced pipeline.

5. **Protect focus and capacity.**
   - Prioritize ColdIQ/core product campaigns this week and next.
   - Avoid spinning up net-new campaign lanes until the core portfolio has launch/reporting rhythm.
