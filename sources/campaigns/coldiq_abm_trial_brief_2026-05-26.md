## What ColdIQ Appears To Run

ColdIQ's public ABM model is not "run LinkedIn ads" plus "send outbound" as separate workstreams. Their model is a coordinated account-based system:

1. Define TAM and target-account list.
2. Segment/tier the TAL by fit, intent, persona, and account priority.
3. Build signal infrastructure around account engagement.
4. Run LinkedIn ads/content to create familiarity and detect account-level intent.
5. Use outbound to convert warm or strategically important accounts into conversations.
6. Feed replies, meetings, CRM status, and ad engagement back into the next campaign cycle.

Their AirOps case study frames the motion as:

- TAM to TAL.
- Signal stack.
- Coordinated touches.
- MQL/SQL movement.
- Qualified deals.

The important operating idea for Portico: accounts, not channels, should be the unit of work. LinkedIn ads, email, LinkedIn sequences, website visits, webinars, and HubSpot stage movement should all describe the same target-account journey.

## ColdIQ's Public Playbook

### 1. LinkedIn ads are the warm-up layer

ColdIQ favors LinkedIn ads for awareness, trust, and intent collection, especially on narrow account lists. Their public ABM examples emphasize:

- Thought Leader Ads and Document Ads for top-of-funnel and account familiarity.
- Consistent exposure to a defined TAL, often 500 to 1,000 accounts, instead of loose broad targeting.
- Campaigns split by persona or department so engagement becomes a signal about which buying group is active.
- Conservative initial budgets optimized for engagement from ICP accounts, not immediate demo conversion.

For Portico, this means LinkedIn should not be judged only on form fills. It should create visibility into which schools, campuses, and buying groups are warming up.

### 2. Outbound is the conversion layer

ColdIQ's public workflow uses outbound after account warming and scoring:

- Find companies engaging with LinkedIn campaigns.
- Qualify them in Clay by industry, company size, CRM status, and engagement depth.
- Find 2-3 contacts in the department/persona that actually engaged.
- Verify emails through a waterfall before sequencing.
- Launch coordinated email and LinkedIn outreach.
- Sync source tags and campaign engagement into CRM.

Their sequence philosophy is multi-touch without feeling like spam. Email handles scale. LinkedIn creates additional familiarity and alternate response paths. Replies and meetings feed back into retargeting and lookalike logic.

### 3. Signals determine the next move

ColdIQ's strongest ABM material is signal-driven:

- LinkedIn ad engagement.
- Organic/social engagement.
- Website visits.
- Webinar engagement.
- Job changes.
- Account news.
- Hiring signals.
- CRM status.
- Existing pipeline or customer status.

Signals control whether an account stays in awareness, moves into consideration with proof assets, or gets a direct-response ask. For Portico, this maps well to schools that show financial aid, compliance, clinical accreditation, attendance, or expansion signals.

### 4. Deliverability and infrastructure are core product

ColdIQ repeatedly treats deliverability as a first-class part of outbound:

- Multiple domains and inboxes.
- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
- Warm-up before real campaigns.
- Rotated mailbox volume.
- Email verification before sequencing.
- Bounce-rate protection.

This matters for Portico because the trial includes 20 domains and 40 inboxes. The domain strategy should be approved carefully so we protect the Portico brand while allowing ColdIQ to safely test at volume.

## Portico Trial Strategy

The trial should be run as a focused 3-4 month experiment, not a general outbound retainer. The success criteria should be whether ColdIQ can create a repeatable TAL-based outbound plus LinkedIn ABM system that Portico can measure in HubSpot.

Recommended trial lanes:

1. Financial Aid / OB3 readiness.
2. Attendance and Skills / student experience plus compliance.
3. Trajecsys Clinical Management / accreditation and clinical documentation.

Do not blend these into one generic Portico campaign. They have different buyers, pains, proof, triggers, and sales motions.

### Lane 1: Financial Aid / OB3

Target:

- Enterprise career schools.
- CEO, President, CFO, COO, VP/Director of Financial Aid, VP Enrollment, Compliance.

Best triggers:

- OBBBA/OB3 uncertainty.
- HCM1/HCM2.
- FA staffing gaps.
- Hiring for FA/compliance roles.
- New campus/program growth.
- Audit/reconciliation pressure.
- Clock-hour, non-term, hybrid, or complex academic models.

Message:

- Portico combines financial aid software, managed services, and compliance support so career schools can reduce manual work, move faster, and stay ready for regulatory change.

Best offers:

- OB3 readiness checklist.
- FA risk assessment.
- Input request around OB3 planning.
- Webinar invite or replay.
- CFO-facing business impact brief.

### Lane 2: Attendance and Skills

Target:

- Enterprise career schools with campus-based, clock-hour, hybrid, trade, allied health, or beauty/wellness operations.
- COO, VP Academics, Director of Academics, Director of Compliance, Director of Student Services, IT Director.

Best triggers:

- Campus expansion.
- Retention initiatives.

## Recommended Operating Model

### Month 1: Build and Warm

Portico should require:

- Final TAL for each lane, with named suppression lists.
- HubSpot lists/views for TAL, open opps, customers, competitors, and suppressed contacts.
- Named AE ownership model and reply-routing rules.
- Domain naming options and approval workflow.
- Sender persona list: names, titles, profile pictures, LinkedIn URLs, signatures.
- LinkedIn ad account access and monthly ad budget.
- Creative/copy pack for each lane.
- Measurement plan in HubSpot.

ColdIQ should deliver:

- Email infrastructure: domains, mailboxes, authentication, warm-up plan.
- ICP matrix.
- Clay buildouts.
- Lead scoring model.
- Personalization waterfall.
- Campaign launch calendar.
- Project management hub.

### Month 2: Launch and Learn

Launch 2-5 plays. Recommended first batch:

- FA OB3 input request or webinar/resource play.
- Attendance compliance/student experience play.
- Trajecsys accreditation play.
- Dream-account executive POV play.
- Retargeting or proof campaign for accounts showing engagement.

LinkedIn ads should target the same account universe as outbound. Use awareness and proof assets early. Do not over-index on "book a demo" CTAs before the audience is warm.

### Month 3: Retarget and Convert

Shift account treatment based on signals:

- Cold accounts: keep in awareness and educational content.
- Engaged accounts: serve case studies, proof, product-specific assets, and role-specific follow-ups.
- Warm accounts: direct meeting asks, bottom-of-funnel retargeting, and seller follow-up.

### Month 4: Evaluate and Decide

Before the renewal window, evaluate:

- TAL coverage by product lane.
- Matched audience quality and reach.
- Engagement by account and persona.
- Positive reply quality, not only reply rate.
- Meetings booked and attended.
- MQL/SQL creation.
- Pipeline created and influenced.
- AE feedback on lead relevance.
- Deliverability health.
- Cost per engaged target account.
- Cost per qualified meeting.
- Whether the system is becoming smarter each month.

Non-renewal reminder: because the agreement auto-renews unless written notice is given at least 30 days before the end of the current term, add a decision checkpoint no later than the beginning of month 3.

## Key Watchouts

- The proposal says Portico supplies creatives and copy. ColdIQ's public materials talk about sophisticated messaging, but the contract places quality risk for copy/creative on Portico.
- Multi-AE routing is not confirmed. Resolve before launch, because routing errors can damage seller trust quickly.
- Domain cost language is ambiguous against the package/setup-fee language. Clarify early.
- Do not let ColdIQ combine FA, Attendance, and Trajecsys into one undifferentiated higher-ed message.
- Do not judge LinkedIn on lead forms alone. The model is account warming, engagement capture, and outbound conversion.
- Make sure HubSpot is the source of truth. If ad engagement, ColdIQ sequences, replies, and meetings do not sync cleanly, the trial will be hard to evaluate.
- Add renewal governance now. Four months is enough to learn, but the auto-renewal notice window arrives before the final month is complete.
