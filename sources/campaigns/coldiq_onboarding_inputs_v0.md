## ColdIQ Research Summary

ColdIQ appears to work best when a client gives them a clear account-based system, not just a list of generic leads. Their public materials emphasize:

- Sharp ICP definition and segmentation.
- Trigger-based prospect lists.
- Multi-channel execution across cold email, LinkedIn outreach, LinkedIn Ads, content, and CRM workflows.
- LinkedIn Ads as a warm-up layer and outbound as a conversion layer.
- Account-level engagement tracking so the next touch changes based on what the account did.
- Short, punchy copy with proof, relevance, and a helpful asset or offer.
- Weekly optimization based on market feedback, engagement, and pipeline impact.

## Best-Fit Account Profile

### Enterprise / Portico

Best-fit institutions:

- U.S.-based career-focused institutions.
- Private, for-profit schools are the strongest fit.
- 250 to 5,000 students.
- Allied health, trade, beauty/wellness, nursing, and career education institutions.
- Multi-location or growing schools.
- Schools with Title IV exposure and operational/compliance complexity.
- Institutions where financial aid, attendance, student engagement, or student operations are visible pain points.

Strong indicators:

- New campus or location growth.
- Acquisitions or mergers.
- Leadership changes in financial aid, admissions, academics, compliance, student services, nursing, or CIO/IT.
- Public regulatory or compliance pressure, including HCM1/HCM2 references.
- Earnings announcements or growth announcements.
- Expansion into new programs, modalities, or geographies.
- Use of legacy or fragmented tools.

### Clinical / Trajecsys

Best-fit institutions/programs:

- U.S.-based nursing and allied health programs.
- Nursing programs with 75+ students.
- Allied health programs with 15+ students.
- Programs managing multiple clinical sites, competencies, evaluations, or accreditation documentation.
- Programs preparing for ACEN, BON, or similar accreditation/review cycles.
- Programs currently relying on paper, spreadsheets, or fragmented clinical tools.

Relevant program types:

- Nursing.
- Respiratory therapy.
- Radiologic technology.
- Diagnostic medical sonography.
- Surgical technology.
- Veterinary technology.
- Dental assisting/hygiene.
- Medical assisting.
- Paramedic/EMT/EMS.
- Pharmacy technician.
- Physical therapy.
- Occupational therapy.
- Clinical laboratory science.
- Other allied health programs with clinical tracking requirements.

## Not A Fit

- Institutions outside the U.S.
- Almost entirely online student populations, especially where in-person attendance, clinical tracking, or campus-based operations are not meaningful.
- Institutions without career, vocational, allied health, trade, beauty/wellness, nursing, or workforce-oriented programs.
- Very small institutions without sufficient operational complexity, except for clinical programs that meet the Trajecsys thresholds.
- Schools with no Title IV/financial aid need for the FA motion.
- Programs with no clinical documentation, accreditation, time/attendance, competency, or site-management burden for the Trajecsys motion.

Named example:

- DeVry University: not a fit because the student population is almost entirely online.

## Buying Triggers

Use these to build Clay/LinkedIn Sales Navigator/intent logic.

### Cross-Product Triggers

- New locations or campus expansion.
- Mergers and acquisitions.
- Executive leadership changes in admissions, financial aid, academics, nursing, compliance, CIO/IT, student services, or operations.
- Growth from earnings announcements.
- Compliance flags around HCM1 or HCM2.
- New program launches.
- Hiring for financial aid, admissions, compliance, registrar, student services, nursing, allied health, or clinical coordination roles.
- Conference attendance or sponsorship around FASFAA, NASFAA, CECU, ACEN, ASRT Ed, OADN, AARC, SDMS, AACN, ASAHP, CAPPS, CSPEN, COE, FAPSC.

### Financial Aid Triggers

- OBBBA / OB3 planning or uncertainty.
- Financial aid staffing gaps.
- High-volume enrollment periods.
- Hiring for Director of Financial Aid, financial aid processors, compliance roles, or VP Enrollment.
- Mentions of FAFSA, ISIR, COD, NSLDS, Title IV, reconciliation, audit findings, clock-hour, non-term, or hybrid programs.
- Schools using or evaluating PowerFAIDS, Regent Education, Anthology Financial Aid, FAME, ECM, Global Financial Aid Services, Inceptia, or similar.

### Attendance Triggers

- Campus expansion or new physical locations.
- Clock-hour, hybrid, asynchronous, or multi-campus operations.
- Student retention or engagement initiatives.
- Compliance initiatives around LDA, attendance verification, or audit-ready records.
- Interest in student mobile app, smart nudges, payments, or student experience improvements.
- Existing manual attendance, paper rosters, SIS-only attendance, or disconnected student engagement tools.

### Clinical Management / Trajecsys Triggers

- Nursing or allied health accreditation cycles.
- New clinical partnerships or clinical site growth.
- Program director, dean, or clinical coordinator hiring.
- Mentions of ACEN, BON, OADN, COARC, SDMS, CAAHEP, or program outcomes.
- Manual clinical logs, paper checkoffs, spreadsheet tracking, competency documentation, or preceptor/evaluation workflows.
- Existing usage or evaluation of Exxat, Typhon, Platinum Planner, MedHub/E*Value, Clinical Trac, Project Concert, Fisdap, or MyClinicalExchange.

Pain:

- Processing slows down, compliance risk rises, and students wait longer.

- Portico Financial Aid Services can act as an extension of the team with expert support and fast turnaround.

Use with context:

- "Integrated platform" should be framed as integrated/modular, not "one fully unified product for every workflow today."
- "AI" should be tied to concrete support, automation, or efficiency use cases, not broad hype.
- "Compliance" should be positioned as support, automation, audit-readiness, and risk reduction, not a legal guarantee.

Avoid:

- Overpromising full product integration.
- Implying Portico replaces every institutional system immediately.
- Saying "guaranteed compliance."
- Leading with broad platform claims when the campaign is for FA, Attendance, or Trajecsys.
