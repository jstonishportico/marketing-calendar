## Shared voice rules

- Peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-buyer.
- Short, specific, and operational.
- Do not force a demo ask in the first touch.
- Ask who owns the work, whether the issue is on their list, or whether a short resource would be useful.
- Avoid broad platform promises. Lead with the product lane that matches the account or persona.
- Use `{{ contact.firstname }}`, `{{ company.name }}`, and `{{ sender.firstname }}` as placeholders.

## Personalization hooks for ColdIQ

Use these sparingly. One relevant trigger line is better than a long custom paragraph.

### Attendance

- New campus or location.
- Hybrid, asynchronous, externship, or clock-hour programs.
- Hiring in student services, academics, compliance, or operations.
- Mentions of retention, LDA, engagement verification, attendance policy, externship hours, or Title IV recordkeeping.

Example:

I saw {{ company.name }} has several hands-on programs where attendance records likely touch both student support and compliance.

### Financial Aid

- OBBBA/OB3 planning.
- Hiring financial aid or compliance staff.
- Public audit, HCM, regulatory, or accreditation pressure.
- High-volume enrollment period.
- Clock-hour or non-term academic model.

Example:

I saw {{ company.name }} runs programs where financial aid operations have to handle regulatory change and non-traditional academic calendars at the same time.

### Clinical Management

- Nursing or allied health program growth.
- Program director, dean, or clinical coordinator hiring.
- Accreditation cycle or site visit.
- New clinical partnerships or additional sites.
- Mentions of ACEN, BON, OADN, CoARC, CAAHEP, SDMS, ASRT, or program outcomes.

Example:

I saw {{ company.name }} has clinical programs where student time, competencies, sites, and accreditation evidence all have to line up.

## Notes for LinkedIn ads alignment

The ads and outbound should sound related, not identical.

### Attendance ad angle

Manual attendance does not only create admin work. It delays the moment when staff know a student is off pace.

### Financial Aid ad angle

Regulatory change is hard enough. Financial aid operations should not also depend on manual workarounds.

### Clinical ad angle

Clinical programs should not have to rebuild the record every time an accreditor asks for proof.
