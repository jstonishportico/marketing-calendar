## Offer strategy

Do not ask for a meeting in Email 1.

Email 1 should offer a useful, specific resource and ask if they want it.

Email 2 should offer a second reason the resource is relevant. If there is a live webinar, use that as the second offer. If not, use the strongest proof asset.

Email 3 should ask whether to send it to the right owner or step back cleanly.

Recommended first offers:

- Financial Aid gets the OBBBA checklist or webinar invite.
- Attendance gets the Unitek attendance story, more info about Portico Attendance, or an attendance recordkeeping checklist.
- Clinical Management gets the Trajecsys clinical recordkeeping examples by modality or the SPE Tracker material.

## Trajecsys ROI angles to workshop

These should not all go into outbound. Use them to build a stronger one-pager or reply asset.

### Conservative time-savings model

If a coordinator saves 10 hours per week during a 32-week academic year, that is 320 hours back.

At 2,080 working hours per year, that is about 0.15 FTE.

If two coordinators save that amount, the program gets about 640 hours back, or about 0.31 FTE.

This is a safe way to translate weekly time savings into staffing capacity without overclaiming payroll savings.

### Accreditation-response model

A program does not need to save money every week for the value to be real.

If accreditation documentation takes weeks to assemble during a review, the value is risk reduction, continuity, and staff capacity at the exact moment the program cannot afford confusion.

This is where Trajecsys has the strongest story.

### Faculty-turnover model

When clinical records live with a coordinator instead of in a system, turnover becomes a compliance risk.

Trajecsys can be positioned around continuity. The record stays with the program when people change roles.

### Suggested proof asset

Create a one-page Trajecsys proof piece with three sections.

1. What programs save weekly.
2. What programs avoid during accreditation.
3. What happens when clinical records survive staff turnover.

Use exact customer language from the testimonial library, then add a small time-savings calculation.
