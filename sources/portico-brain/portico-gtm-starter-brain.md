# Portico GTM Starter Brain

Last assembled: 2026-06-16 from local Portico context files. This is a starter context file to give Codex before Portico marketing work. It is not a replacement for live HubSpot, SharePoint, Teams, Outlook, GitHub, or content calendar data.

## Business Frame

Portico is a career education technology and services company formed from Campus Ivy, CourseKey, Verity, and Trajecsys. The public story is an integrated, modular suite across the student lifecycle. The GTM reality is staged: sell the products that work today while building trust in the broader platform vision.

The growth job is immediate demand for sell-ready products plus future demand for the platform.

## Campaign Lanes

Financial Aid:

- Job: capture urgent demand from OB3/OBBBA anxiety and compliance complexity.
- Audience: financial aid leaders, CFOs, presidents, compliance leaders.
- Angle: "Ready for OB3. Ready for what comes next."
- Proof: OB3 resource hub, Q&A webinars, readiness content, managed services expertise, 100% file review, one-business-day turnaround.

Clinical Management / Trajecsys:

- Job: drive high-volume programmatic pipeline and event conversion.
- Audience: nursing/allied health program directors, deans, clinical coordinators, accreditation leaders.
- Angle: "Accreditation without the scramble."
- Proof: 3,000+ programs, 70+ modalities, SPE Tracker, ACEN/BON relevance, fast implementation, strong support.

Attendance & Skills:

- Job: land-and-expand entry point into enterprise target accounts.
- Audience: academics, compliance, student services, operations, finance.
- Angle: "Student experience meets compliance."
- Proof: patented GPS, student mobile app, real-time audit-ready data, 3.5% average retention lift.

Platform vision:

- Job: executive trust and future demand.
- Audience: owners, presidents, CFOs, COOs, multi-campus leaders.
- Angle: "From Here to Career."
- Use The Commons, Momentum, executive briefs, case studies, and platform POV content.

## Voice Guardrails

- Be clear, transparent, useful, and specific.
- Avoid generic B2B language and unsupported claims.
- Sound like a field peer with judgment, not a vendor.
- Use Portico voice and design standards for customer-facing work.
