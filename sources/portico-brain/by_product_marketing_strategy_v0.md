# By-Product Marketing Strategy V0

Date: 2026-05-20

## Strategy Thesis

Portico should run a focused portfolio strategy: generate immediate demand for sell-ready products while using the platform story to create executive trust and cross-sell momentum.

The mistake to avoid is treating every product equally. The growth plan should allocate campaign intensity by GTM readiness, revenue potential, sales motion, and market urgency.

## Product Focus Ladder

### Tier 1: Campaign-Led Growth

These products deserve full campaign architecture: ICP, message, offer, landing page, paid support, outbound, events, sales enablement, and measurement.

- Financial Aid Software and Services.

### Tier 2: Strategic Support

These products get selective marketing support when tied to strategic accounts, bundles, or platform expansion.

- SIS / Portico Student.
- Payments.
- CRM / Portico Recruit.

### Tier 3: Product Communications

These products get release notes, customer updates, launch announcements, enablement, and cross-sell mentions unless promoted into a campaign lane.

- Interventions / Smart Nudges.
- Document Management / Paperless Campus.
- Pathways.
- Community.
- AI support agents.
- Data layer / Lumina concepts.

## Core Motions

### 1. Target Account Demand

Use sales-identified target accounts as the paid and outbound universe.

- Segment by target product: FA, Attendance, Clinical.
- Build audience by job title at those accounts.
- Create product-specific landing pages for each campaign.
- Retarget engaged visitors with proof assets and event/webinar offers.
- Feed all engagement back into HubSpot lists and sales views.

### 2. Event Conversion

Treat each event as a campaign with four stages.

- Pre-event: list build, account targeting, meeting ask, speaker/session promotion.
- Onsite: scheduled meetings, QR capture, executive moments, field notes.
- 48-hour follow-up: segmented by meeting, attendee type, no-show, and content interest.
- Post-event: webinar replay, content recap, sales sequence, targeted asset.

Priority events:

- FASFAA and NASFAA for Financial Aid.
- ACEN, ASRT Ed, OADN, AARC, AEIRS, CAAHEP, AVTE, SDMS, AACN, ASAHP for Clinical.
- CECU, CAPPS, CCST, CSPEN, COE, FAPSC for platform, Attendance, and enterprise.

### 3. Authority Content

Use The Commons and newsletters to make Portico feel like the operator that understands career education better than generic edtech vendors.

- Financial Aid: OB3 guidance, risk assessments, compliance explanations, implementation checklists.
- Clinical: accreditation burden, SPE tracking, clinical site coordination, program outcomes.
- Attendance: retention, student experience, audit-readiness, LDA/engagement verification.
- Platform: from enrollment to employment, career-school growth, workforce outcomes.

### 4. Sales Enablement

Every campaign should create seller-ready proof, not only public content.

- Persona-specific talk tracks.
- Objection handling.
- Competitor comparisons.
- One-page ROI narrative.
- Follow-up email snippets.
- Discovery questions.
- Buying committee maps.

## Measurement Model

Source of truth:

- HubSpot: deal operations, target account engagement, lists, sequences, next steps.
- Grow: product-line reporting metrics.
- Content calendar: campaign timing.
- Sales feedback channels: market intel, win/loss, objections, customer voice.

Campaign metrics:

- Target-account reach.
- Known contact creation.
- Landing page conversion.
- Webinar/event registrations.
- Meeting requests.
- MQL to opportunity conversion.
- Pipeline created and influenced.
- Product-line pipeline.
- Deal velocity.
- Closed-won revenue.

Operating rhythm:

- Weekly product campaign review.
- Monthly product-line scorecard.
- Event retro within one week of each major event.
- Quarterly product marketing strategy refresh.
