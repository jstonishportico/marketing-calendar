## Current Business Frame

Portico is a career education technology and services company created from Campus Ivy, CourseKey, Verity, and Trajecsys. The public brand promise is an integrated, modular suite spanning the student lifecycle, but current GTM reality is that the suite is still being integrated. Messaging should be careful: sell the products that work today while laddering up to the platform vision.

The internal growth goal is immediate demand for "what we can sell" plus future demand for "the vision."

Current sell-ready priorities:

- Financial Aid Software, formerly Campus Ivy CORE.
- Financial Aid Managed Services.
- Trajecsys Clinical Management, still a standalone-loved brand but now powered by Portico.
- Attendance & Skills, formerly CourseKey, positioned as the land-and-expand entry point.

Secondary or release-note-oriented products:

- Portico Recruit / CRM.
- Portico Student / SIS.
- Payments.
- Interventions / Smart Nudges.
- Document Management / Paperless Campus.
- Pathways, Community, data layer, AI support agents, and broader platform packaging.

## GTM Reality

## Product-Level Priorities

### Financial Aid

Financial Aid is the top GTM priority because it has the strongest motion, highest leverage, and best combination of ACV and fit.

Core buyer problems:

- Staffing and expertise gaps.
- Compliance risk.
- Seasonal workload spikes.
- Slow processing times.
- Audit and reconciliation challenges.
- Manual or disconnected FAFSA, ISIR, document verification, packaging, and disbursement processes.
- Academic model complexity across clock hour, credit hour, term-based, non-term, and hybrid programs.

Portico position:

- Combines automation, expert services, and compliance support.
- Software plus services in one model.
- Software included with services.
- One-business-day turnaround for ISIR corrections, COD updates, and verification.
- 100% file review and stronger compliance coverage.
- Built for non-traditional and clock-hour institutions.

OBBBA / OB3 is the live market catalyst:

- Institutions are stressed about OBBBA impact.
- Portico is leading guidance through resource hub, webinars, product updates, newsletters, and event content.
- Product work includes OB3 Loan and Pell changes, OB3 data contract updates, financial aid automation transparency, AI integration for managed services efficiency, and annual/OB3 regulatory changes.

Key FA campaign assets/motions:

- OB3 Resource Hub.
- OB3 checklist, WIP.
- FA Campaign Landing Page.
- FA Risk Assessment.
- OB3 product videos.
- OBBBA Live Q&A webinars.
- FASFAA, CECU, NASFAA, and later CSPEN, FAPSC, CCST, CAPPS, COE.

### Clinical Management / Trajecsys

Clinical Management is the second priority because Trajecsys is loved, proven, and easier to sell.

Core buyer problems:

- Paper or spreadsheet-driven clinical tracking.
- Manual hours, competencies, evaluations, and attendance tracking.
- Accreditation burden with fragmented data.
- Faculty and staff burnout.
- Multi-site and multi-campus complexity.
- Risk of data loss, compliance issues, or failed accreditation reviews.

Portico position:

- Trajecsys is now a Portico brand.
- Used by 3,000+ healthcare programs across 70+ modalities.
- Built by educators, for educators.
- Centralized clinical recordkeeping from attendance to accreditation.
- Fast deployment and easy scaling.
- Strong support and implementation expertise.

Differentiators:

- Time, attendance, clinical hours, competencies, evaluations, and reporting in one system.
- Patented SPE Tracker directly ties activity to accreditation standards.
- Audit-ready reporting for ACEN, BON, and program outcome needs.
- Geofencing, no app required, secure and compliant.
- OADN partnership and relevance to nursing/ADN programs.

Priority audiences:

- Nursing program directors.
- Deans of nursing and allied health.
- Academic and clinical coordinators.
- Accreditation and assessment leaders.
- Allied health programs, from respiratory therapy to radiologic technology, surgical tech, veterinary tech, and more.

Key campaign/events:

- SPE Tracker one-pager.
- Testimonial videos.
- Clinical Management landing page.
- Vet Tech webinar.
- ACEN, ASRT Ed, OADN, AARC, AEIRS, CAAHEP, AVTE, SDMS, FNCE, AACN, ASAHP.
- Partnership marketing: Cisive podcast, KPA recap, SDMS EdPro Library.

### Attendance & Skills

Attendance is the bigger land-and-expand play. It is the easiest entry point into institutions but has been under-supported from a content and campaign standpoint.

Core buyer problems:

- Manual attendance and skills tracking.
- Retention issues due to lack of real-time student data.
- LDA, engagement verification, and audit requirements.
- Student experience gaps with outdated SIS portals.
- Disconnected systems for attendance, skills, surveys, interventions, and payments.

Portico position:

- Mobile-first attendance and skills app for career schools.
- Students manage attendance, assignments, schedules, and skills progress.
- Staff get less chasing and fewer reminders.
- Secure check-in methods include biometrics, QR codes, sound, and patented GPS.
- Automated nudges and reminders support retention and compliance.
- Real-time audit-ready data.

Known value claims:

- Improve retention by 3.5% on average.
- Reduce manual work and compliance risk.
- Student mobile app as an engagement and experience wedge.
- Combined attendance, skills, payments, and engagement story.

Attendance needs a real campaign lane once ColdIQ and paid LinkedIn are running.

### CRM, SIS, Payments, Interventions, Document Management

These products are part of the platform story but are not the core campaign focus right now.

Current marketing mode:

- Select strategic support for major SIS opportunities.

SIS is a controlled strategic motion, not broad GTM. Key target accounts named in kickoff include Keiser, Ember, Education Affiliates, and ECPI.

- Buyer titles: CEO, President, CFO, VP/Director of Financial Aid, Compliance Officer, COO, VP Enrollment/Student Affairs.

Attendance:

- Career and trade schools.
- Allied health, beauty/cosmetology, emergency services, clock-hour, multi-campus institutions.
- Buyers: CEO, COO, CFO, CTO, VP/Director Academics, Director Compliance, Director Student Services, IT.

Clinical Management:

- Nursing and allied health programs, often smaller than whole-school targets.
- Nursing ideal threshold: 75+ students.
- Allied health threshold: 15+ students.
- Buyers: program directors, deans of nursing/allied health, clinical coordinators, accreditation/assessment leaders.

Voice:

- Clear, transparent, and honest.

Visual system:

- Primary type: Proxima Nova.

- Brand pattern should remain subtle; bright accents occupy no more than 20 percent of secondary pattern compositions.

## Growth OS Implications

This should become a portfolio growth operating system, not a collection of disconnected campaigns.

Core operating layers:

- Product priority map: what is ready to sell, what is strategic, what is release-note-only.
- Source-of-truth map: HubSpot for deal operations, Grow for trustworthy product-line reporting, content calendar for campaign calendar, target account lists for ABM.
- Campaign factory: every priority product gets ICP, buying committee, pain, proof, offer, channel, assets, owner, KPIs, and next action.
- Event ROI system: pre-event target list, meeting goals, field enablement, onsite capture, 48-hour follow-up, post-event sequence, pipeline attribution.
- ABM system: account-specific landing pages, paid LinkedIn by job title at target accounts, outbound sequences, gifting or micro-events for high-value accounts.
- Content system: The Commons, newsletters, webinars, resource hubs, SME LinkedIn, product videos, case studies, and proof assets.
- Learning loop: plus/delta, win/loss, NPS, sales feedback, customer voice, and campaign performance.

## Initial Product Marketing Strategy Shape

Recommended campaign lanes:

1. Financial Aid: "OB3 Readiness / Compliance Over Chaos"
   - Primary goal: urgent demand and trust capture.
   - Audience: FA leaders, CFOs, compliance, presidents.
   - Offer stack: resource hub, checklist, risk assessment, Q&A webinar, product videos, services/software consult.
   - Proof: readiness, product updates, compliance expertise, speed, automation, managed services depth.

2. Clinical Management: "Accreditation Without the Scramble"
   - Primary goal: high-volume programmatic pipeline and event conversion.
   - Audience: nursing/allied health program leaders and clinical coordinators.
   - Offer stack: SPE Tracker one-pager, program-specific landing pages, webinar/event sequences, testimonial clips.
   - Proof: 3,000+ programs, 70+ modalities, SPE Tracker, ACEN/BON evidence, time saved, support.

3. Attendance: "Student Experience Meets Compliance"
   - Primary goal: land-and-expand entry point for target accounts.
   - Audience: academics, compliance, student services, operations, finance.
   - Offer stack: paid LinkedIn landing pages, retention/compliance content, product video, ROI calculator or audit-readiness checklist.
   - Proof: patented GPS, student mobile app, real-time visibility, 3.5% retention lift, faster implementation.

4. Platform Vision: "From Here to Career"
   - Primary goal: future demand, executive trust, cross-sell.
   - Audience: owners, presidents, CFOs, COOs, multi-campus leaders.
   - Offer stack: The Commons, Momentum, executive briefings, case studies, platform narrative.
   - Proof: integrated yet modular suite, career-school specificity, customer experience, workforce outcomes.

5. Release/Launch Layer:
   - CRM, SIS, Payments, Interventions, and Document Management should get product update, customer comms, enablement, and strategic-account support unless leadership promotes one into a primary GTM lane.
